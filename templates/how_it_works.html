How FirstGig Works

FirstGig provides a structured, safe bridge between college students seeking real-world experience and businesses looking for verified, capable talent.

01

Create Your Profile

Sign up and highlight your collegiate background, technical skills (Python, React, Figma, SQL, etc.), academic branch, and interests.

02

Verify College Institutional Email

Verify your official university or collegiate email address to unlock your 🎓 Verified Student badge, giving prospective clients immediate confidence.

03

Find & Apply to Your First Gig

Browse starter-friendly micro-projects tagged specifically for collegiate beginners. Use our rule-based matching to apply where your skills score 85%+.

04

Deliver Milestones Under Mock Escrow

Work confidently knowing the client's simulated payment is safely locked in Mock Escrow before you begin. Track milestones and submit code or design links.

05

Receive Verified Review & Simulated Payout

Upon approval, simulated funds are credited to your balance and your client writes an authentic review tied permanently to your FirstGig profile.

06

Build Irrefutable Commercial Reputation

Watch your Reliability Rating, On-Time Score, and Career Progression Tier advance automatically in our live database.

07

Unlock Higher-Budget Freelance Opportunities

Graduate from Beginner gigs to Medium and Advanced projects (₹10,000+) as a Trusted or Pro Freelancer with a showcase-ready portfolio.

01

Post Your Gig with Clear Requirements

State your deliverables, required competencies, deadline days, and budget in Indian Rupees (₹). Categorize difficulty as Beginner, Medium, or Advanced.

02

Review Verified Student Candidates

Compare applicant proposals side-by-side with institutional verification status, career progression tier, past review scores, and public portfolios.

03

Select Best Candidate

Accept an applicant with one click. FirstGig automatically creates a dedicated project workspace with milestone tracking.

04

Simulate Protected Escrow

Funds are simulated and held safely in Mock Escrow. No real money is transferred during this prototype, demonstrating the risk-free escrow architecture.

05

Track Milestone Deliverables

Monitor real-time progress, visual deadline meters (Green / Yellow / Red), and milestone submissions through your client dashboard.

06

Approve or Request Revisions

Review the student's submission links and code. Approve the project or request clear modifications. In case of disagreement, our dispute center handles arbitration.

07

Release Escrow & Review

Releasing simulated escrow marks the project as completed and prompts you to leave a 5-star review that shapes the student's career trajectory.
