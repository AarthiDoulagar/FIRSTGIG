Step-by-Step Workflow

How It Works

A transparent, structured lifecycle designed for student safety and client satisfaction.

01

Create Profile

Highlight your academic degree, technical skills, interests, and GitHub or design links.

02

Verify College Email

Receive your instant 🎓 Verified Student badge to immediately distinguish yourself from unverified candidates.

03

Find First Gig

Browse starter-friendly micro-projects tagged specifically for college talent and submit simple proposals.

04

Complete Project & Milestones

Track deadlines with visual indicators and submit deliverables through the workspace.

05

Receive Verified Review

Clients approve work, mock escrow payment is simulated, and a genuine review is locked into your profile.

06

Build Reputation

Your reliability score, on-time percentage, and career level advance automatically in the database.

07

Unlock Better Projects

Progress to Rising and Verified tiers to receive personalized, higher-budget gig recommendations.

01

Post a Gig

Define your deliverables, deadline, category, and budget in Indian Rupees (₹).

02

Review Student Profiles

Inspect college credentials, verified career tiers, reliability percentages, and genuine past reviews.

03

Select Best Student

Accept an applicant's proposal with one click to initiate the dedicated project workspace.

04

Use Mock Escrow

Simulated funds are safely locked in escrow so the student knows payment is secured upon completion.

05

Track Project Progress

Monitor real-time milestone completions and deadline countdowns.

06

Approve Work

Review the student's submission, request adjustments if needed, or approve to release simulated escrow.

07

Review Student

Leave a 5-star rating and testimonial that cements the student's verified track record.
