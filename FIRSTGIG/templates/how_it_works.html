How FirstGig works

A progression system, not a generic freelance board.

Students

  1. Register with college details and skills.
  2. Complete mock college-email verification → 🎓 Verified Student.
  3. Browse the marketplace. Rule-based recommendations match skills, interests, budget band, and difficulty.
  4. Apply once per gig with a proposal.
  5. When hired, work against milestones while budget sits in mock escrow.
  6. Submit work. Client approves → COMPLETED → payment released (simulated) → reviews unlock.
  7. Completed gigs raise your level automatically. Cancelled/refunded work does not count.

Clients

  1. Post a gig with budget, deadline, skills, and milestone count.
  2. Compare applicants: level, rating, reliability, on-time %, portfolio.
  3. Accept one student — others are rejected, project + held escrow are created.
  4. Approve, request changes, extend a deadline, or open a dispute.
  5. Overdue work is flagged. Refund is never automatic; admin can resolve.
